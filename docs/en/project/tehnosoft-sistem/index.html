Platform: the problem

The country’s largest e-commerce platform had hit a ceiling: at 10,000 concurrent users pages were slow, search across a 1.5M+ product catalog was heavy, and peak sale days turned into a risk for the whole network.

Platform: what I did

  • Layered Redis caching — from catalog objects to ready-made page fragments.
  • Elasticsearch with a reworked query and index strategy, replacing the previous search.
  • Heavy operations moved into async queues — Kafka and RabbitMQ.
  • Horizontal scaling in Kubernetes with CI/CD and Sentry monitoring.
  • Scalable SEO infrastructure: crawler traffic and indexing of 1.5M+ products.
  • A delivery system built from scratch, with real-time order tracking.
  • AI Visual Search: product lookup by photo, built in-house.
  • An AI shopping assistant automating customer support.

Platform: the outcome

  • The platform holds 100,000+ concurrent users — ten times the previous limit.
  • Individual page speed improved by up to 5×.
  • AI features run in production on real traffic, not in a demo environment.

Self-checkout: the problem

Physical stores could only sell what sat on the shelf, and the checkout queue ate conversion. The company needed a self-service till that accepts cards and cash, prints a fiscal receipt, and sells the entire network catalog.

Self-checkout: what I did

I designed, built and shipped the whole system as its sole engineer — from hardware protocols to running the fleet in production.

  • Integration with bank terminals (Arcus), fiscal printers (Datecs) and bill acceptors (CashCode) over SerialPort.
  • An Electron terminal client on top of the platform’s shared backend.
  • QR-based customer data entry — the buyer types nothing by hand.
  • On-the-spot product scanning and ordering from the full network catalog.
  • Monitoring and healthchecks across all 41 terminals, remote diagnostics and updates.

Self-checkout: the outcome

  • 41 terminals across 32+ stores running in production.
  • Checkout takes around 10 seconds.
  • Terminal orders tripled; more than half of in-store purchases go through self-service.
  • Any location sells the full 1.5M+ catalog instead of only its shelf stock.
  • Outages under once a month network-wide, average recovery 2–5 minutes.
